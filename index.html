WHO IT'S FOR

Wherever you're starting from, there's a seat at this board

Grandchildren & kids

Patient, encouraging lessons with animated pieces and simple language — never condescending, never rushed.

Seniors & retirees

Large text, a real recorded voice, and no fussy interface — pick the game back up at your own pace.

Absolute beginners

Never played before? Grandpa starts at square one — how each piece moves, one square at a time.

Players ready to improve

Already know the rules? Sharpen tactics, learn openings, and get honest feedback on every move you make.

HOW IT WORKS

Three easy steps, every time you sit down to play

1

Set up your board

Place the pieces by hand, or snap a photo of your real board and let Grandpa read it for you.

2

Make your move

Drag a piece to its square. Grandpa reacts to every move — what worked, and what to watch for next.

3

Ask anything, anytime

"How does the knight move?" "Was that a good move?" Grandpa always has a warm, honest answer.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Everything a player needs, nothing they don't

How the pieces move

Square-by-square demonstrations for every piece — including the knight's tricky L-shape.

Tactics, shown on the board

Forks, pins and threats drawn right on the board with arrows, not just words.

Photo board reader

Take a picture of your real chess set and Grandpa sets up the board on screen to match.

A real grandpa's voice

Warm, unhurried coaching in a real recorded voice — not a robot.

Openings and defenses

Grandpa names the opening after you play it, and suggests a solid defense when you play Black.

Seven languages

English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Portuguese and Vietnamese.
